Appendix

Method and definitions

How the 280 answers were collected

  • 7 luxury real estate vendor, buyer, and property management questions (Best Luxury Agency, $5M+ Prestige Sales, Boutique Design-Led Agency, Off-Market Prestige Sales, Luxury Buyer's Advocacy, Penthouse & Waterfront Specialists, Boutique Executive Leasing) × 8 Australian markets = 56 market-intent pairs.
  • Each pair was evaluated across 5 live AI answer engines (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity Sonar, Gemini 2.5 Flash, and Claude Sonnet 4.6) for N = 280 total empirical answers.
  • 56 matching Australian Google SERP queries (depth 20), 8 Google Maps 20-pack audits, and 14 individual branch GBP CID lookups were executed via DataForSEO.
  • 200 URLs on whitefoxrealestate.com.au were mapped and 21 core & branch pages were scraped via Firecrawl to inspect JSON-LD schema, HTTP status codes, and NAP consistency.

How answers, citations, and econometrics were scored

  • Every luxury real estate agency and buyer's advocate named in an answer was extracted in order of first appearance and resolved to its canonical entity name across 55 tracked Australian prestige agencies.
  • Grounding URLs and search citations were resolved to root domains in pipeline/citations.json (4,194 citations across 741 unique domains).
  • Share of Model Voice (SoMV) is the percentage of answers naming an agency out of 280 nationally (or out of 35 per market), bounded with 95% Wilson confidence intervals.
  • Econometric drivers (β̂ coefficients, standard errors, Wald z-statistics, and odds ratios) were estimated via live Iteratively Reweighted Least Squares (IRLS) logistic regression over the 280 empirical observations in pipeline/ans_full.json.
